Trading is a process,
not a prediction.

Six disciplines carry most of what a trader actually uses — the rest is noise.

Risk management

The part no one enjoys, and every good trader respects.

Position sizing

Risking a small, fixed percentage of capital per trade — often 1–2% — so no single loss threatens the account.

Stop-loss placement

A predefined exit that caps the damage, typically set using ATR or a recent structural level.

Risk-to-reward

Comparing potential loss to potential gain before entering — a 1:2 ratio or better is a common baseline.

Backtesting

Running a strategy against historical data before risking real capital, to see how it would have actually performed.
